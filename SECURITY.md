# Reporting problems

Identify whether a report concerns the original whitepaper, the IEEE paper, commercial desktop 1.1.4, or a particular demonstration release. Include the version, the stated effect and the observed discrepancy.

Report demonstration functional problems in the public [demo Issues](https://github.com/waiyip000/traplesspke-two-boundary-demo/issues), following its [reporting guidance](https://github.com/waiyip000/traplesspke-two-boundary-demo/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Use this research repository's Issues for research-documentation problems. Use disposable synthetic examples. Do not include passwords, reusable private keys, customer messages, unrevealed challenge answers, private commercial source or internal logs containing those materials.

No private disclosure address or response-time commitment is asserted by this repository. Do not post sensitive details publicly to compensate for an unavailable private channel. Use an established private support route if one has been provided to you.

This research repository is now public following the owner's publication instruction. Its pages and Issues are publicly accessible. The separate [demo repository](https://github.com/waiyip000/traplesspke-two-boundary-demo), [downloads](https://github.com/waiyip000/traplesspke-two-boundary-demo/releases/tag/v0.1.2), [manual](https://github.com/waiyip000/traplesspke-two-boundary-demo/blob/main/USER_MANUAL.md) and demo Issues remain public; their availability does not depend on this repository's visibility.

Read [SECURITY_SCOPE.md](SECURITY_SCOPE.md) before interpreting a local round trip or an exposure view as a broader security result.
